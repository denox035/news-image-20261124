# news-image-20261124

One image summing up the world news of Tuesday, 24 November 2026.

Hero story: the Dow Jones closed above 50,000 for the first time
(50,018.70, +0.85%) while the Nasdaq fell 1.48% as Nvidia gave back
Monday's surge. Also covered: Sanofi's $41 billion deal for Insmed,
Virginia and Oregon suing SpaceX over Grok deepfakes, the UK sanctioning
five Myanmar generals after the ICJ genocide ruling, and Israeli strikes
in Gaza. Markets strip: BTC, gold, WTI crude and US consumer confidence.

The poster (`entry.png`, 1024×1024) was drawn programmatically with PIL;
`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies it to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`. Run it twice and the outputs are
byte-identical.

Entry for the make.fun competition `news-image-20261124`.
